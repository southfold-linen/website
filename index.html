What you can count on

Most complaints about linen suppliers come down to one thing: the second order isn't the same as the first. Everything we do is built around fixing that.

  • The same product every order

    A signed golden sample and a written spec for every item, and a batch code on every care label, so any problem traces back to its order.

  • Sheets sorted in seconds

    Coloured hem thread by size: blue for queen, green for king single. Housekeeping can grab the right sheet without unfolding it.

  • Order what you need

    Buy in bundles of 10 and mix items in one carton. No container-sized minimum orders.

  • Made for commercial washing

    Bleach-resistant whites, double-stitched hems and reinforced corners, specified for laundry life rather than shelf appeal.

  • Stock in Melbourne

    From mid-2027 we hold stock locally and deliver within one to two weeks of your order.

  • A free linen count

    We'll count your linen, recommend a stock level for each room and remind you when it's time to reorder.
